# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want GridWise AI to be a focused, respectful place for technical discussion. Participants are expected to be constructive, specific, and considerate of different experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/hossiendehghan989). Reports will be reviewed and handled in good faith. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this standard.
